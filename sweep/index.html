How the pattern is defined here. A level is a price with two or more pivot touches (horizontal) or a line fitted through consecutive pivots (sloped channel). A sweep is a bar whose wick pierces that level and whose close returns inside it within three bars, with a volume thrust and a wick that dominates its body. TSI must be at its extreme band and turning back. Entry is the reclaim close, the stop sits beyond the sweep wick, and the target is the opposite side of the range with a 1:1 floor.

On leverage. Sizing is locked to your leverage setting and never adjusted down — that is a deliberate choice you made. The liquidation price, the distance to it, and whether your stop sits beyond it are shown on every setup as information. At 500× your margin is 0.2% of notional, so a 0.15% adverse move is most of it.

Not financial advice. This is a pattern detector on live public price data. Detected setups are tendencies, not predictions, and the replay statistics are a small sample of recent bars — not an edge you can bank on. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
